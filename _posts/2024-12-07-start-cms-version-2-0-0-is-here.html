Get Ready to Revolutionize Your Content Management!

Hi everyone,

I’m thrilled to announce the release of Start CMS Version 2.0.0, the latest and greatest version of our lightweight content management system! This new version is packed with exciting new features that will make managing your website easier and more enjoyable than ever before.

One of the most exciting new features in Start CMS Version 2.0.0 is the dynamic forms content. With this feature, you can now create custom forms that can capture and store any data you need. Whether you’re looking to create a contact form, a survey, or anything in between, Start CMS has you covered.


Redesigned Dashboard

We’ve also added new widgets to the dashboard, making it even easier to manage your website. With the new widgets, you can quickly see your most recent posts, check on your site’s traffic, and more. And with the new user tracking feature, you can see exactly how your visitors are interacting with your site, giving you valuable insights into how you can improve their experience.

Notifications

Another major new feature in Start CMS Version 2.0.0 is the ability to create albums for your photos and videos. Now, you can organize your media in a way that makes sense to you, making it easier to find what you’re looking for when you need it.


New Calendar

And that’s just the tip of the iceberg! We’ve also added new categories and subcategories for your content, a new events management system, and a whole host of other improvements and optimizations that make Start CMS faster, more reliable, and easier to use than ever before.


Export Data

So, if you’re looking for a lightweight, powerful, and user-friendly content management system, look no further than Start CMS Version 2.0.0. With its powerful features, beautiful interface, and rock-solid performance, it’s the perfect choice for anyone looking to take their website to the next level.

Thank you for choosing Start CMS, and happy building!
