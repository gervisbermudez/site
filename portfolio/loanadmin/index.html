Project details

LoanAdmin Overview is an open-source web application that simplifies sales and income tracking. Built with VueJs and CodeIgniter PHP, it offers real-time data visualization through an intuitive dashboard. Users can customize LoanAdmin to meet their specific needs, making it a versatile tool for managing financial data. The project, initiated on March 1, 2020, is available on GitHub and Figma for collaboration and further development.

Are you tired of manually tracking your sales and incomes? Look no further than LoanAdmin!

Our web application offers a beautifully designed dashboard with a plethora of useful information to help you stay on top of your finances. With LoanAdmin, you can easily monitor your sales and incomes, allowing you to make informed decisions and grow your business.

But that’s not all – LoanAdmin is also an open-source project, meaning you have the flexibility to customize and improve the application to fit your specific needs. Plus, it’s built with VueJs and CodeIgniter PHP, ensuring a smooth and efficient user experience.

Some of the key features of LoanAdmin include:

Detailed sales and income tracking: Keep track of your sales and incomes in real-time, with the ability to view and analyze your data by date, product, and more.

Easy-to-use dashboard: Our intuitive dashboard makes it simple to view and manage your sales and incomes, with clear, concise data visualizations to help you make informed decisions.

Customizable and open-source: As an open-source project, LoanAdmin allows you to tailor the application to your specific needs, and even contribute to its development and improvement.

VueJs and CodeIgniter PHP: Built with VueJs and CodeIgniter PHP, LoanAdmin offers a fast and efficient user experience, ensuring smooth and seamless interactions.

With LoanAdmin, you can easily and effectively manage your sales and incomes, allowing you to focus on growing your business. Give it a try today and experience the benefits for yourself!
